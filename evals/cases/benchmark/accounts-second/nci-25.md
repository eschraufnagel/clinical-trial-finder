I'm 63. I have a T-cell lymphoma, a peripheral T-cell lymphoma, found about two years ago. It had already spread to my lymph nodes and bone marrow when they found it.

I had chemo with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and prednisone. It shrank the lymphoma, but it came back six months later. Then I had chemo with ifosfamide, carboplatin and etoposide, which didn't work. Then I had mogamulizumab, which helped my skin and lymph nodes for a short while, but it stopped working within three months. Now it's growing in lymph nodes at the back of my belly and in my chest. It isn't in my brain or my blood.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have high blood pressure and some kidney disease. My blood work was fine.
