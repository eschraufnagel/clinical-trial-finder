I'm 38. I was diagnosed with cervical cancer about three and a half years ago. They did a hysterectomy, and afterwards I had chemo and radiation together. About two and a half years ago a scan found spots in my lungs, and a biopsy showed the cervical cancer had come back there.

I had chemo with cisplatin and paclitaxel, with pembrolizumab and bevacizumab, and then stayed on pembrolizumab for a little over a year. It stopped working when a spot in my right lung grew. I took a break of a few months, then had tisotumab vedotin for about nine months, until a few weeks ago. A scan after that showed new spots in my liver.

My oncologist at home is starting me on topotecan while I look for a clinical trial. I'm working full-time. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me.
