I'm 75. I have nerve damage in my hands and feet, and nobody knows the cause. I have cancer of the urethra, an adenocarcinoma. It was found after I was in the hospital because the stents in the tubes from both my kidneys got blocked. They replaced the stents and scraped out tissue from my bladder, which showed the cancer.

It was hard for them to tell whether it started in my urinary tract or my bowel. I had a big surgery that removed my bladder and other organs in my pelvis, and they decided it started in my urinary tract. Afterwards I had one dose of chemo with fluorouracil and oxaliplatin, and I couldn't tolerate it. After that the cancer grew, in lymph nodes in the middle of my chest.
