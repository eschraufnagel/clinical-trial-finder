I'm 65. I have uterine cancer, a serous kind. About a year and a half ago I went to the emergency room with three months of belly pain and swelling. They drained fluid from my belly and found cancer cells in it, so it had already spread when they found it. I had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel, with bevacizumab, and during it they did a robotic hysterectomy and took out lymph nodes in my pelvis. I kept going on carboplatin and paclitaxel, which gave me nerve damage, heartburn, hearing loss and a severe rash. When it finished there was no sign of cancer, and I stayed on bevacizumab.

About ten months ago a scan showed new spots in my liver, so it stopped working. I went back on carboplatin, paclitaxel and bevacizumab, which gave me scalp pain and changed my taste, and I started propranolol for high blood pressure during it. A few months later a scan showed more spots in my liver, lymph nodes near my liver, and maybe something in my pelvis, so it stopped working. Then I had pembrolizumab with lenvatinib. My blood counts dropped badly and I needed transfusions, and I got tendinitis in my left arm and a stiff neck. For a while I took lenvatinib alone, then went back on both. A couple of months ago they took out part of my liver. I've just been taken off treatment because the scans showed it growing, so it stopped working.

I'm more tired and get winded doing everyday things, but I'm on my feet all day. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I still get fevers and chills at night; last night I had a high fever that came down with Tylenol and ice packs. I have a dry cough that's better than it was, some bloating, and the numbness in both feet is worse. I also have high blood pressure from bevacizumab, a hiatal hernia, high cholesterol, an underactive thyroid, osteoarthritis and osteoporosis.
