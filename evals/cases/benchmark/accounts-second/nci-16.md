I'm 73. I have high cholesterol, and a kidney condition called medullary sponge kidney that keeps giving me kidney stones; about eight years ago I had surgery to take out a large stone.

I was diagnosed with small cell cancer of the bladder, with some urothelial cancer mixed in, a little over three years ago. Before any surgery I had chemo with cisplatin and etoposide, and the cancer went away. It came back in my bladder about a year ago, found when they scraped out a tumor, and a biopsy showed it had spread to my liver. A bone scan showed a possible spot in my lower spine. I had more chemo with cisplatin and etoposide, and it stopped working. Then I had pembrolizumab for about eight months. It was held at times because it inflamed my lungs. A recent MRI showed the cancer in my liver is growing, so it stopped working.
