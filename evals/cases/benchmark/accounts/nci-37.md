I'm 65. I have endometrial cancer, a serous kind. About a year and a half ago I went to the emergency room with three months of belly pain and swelling, and they drained fluid from my belly that had cancer cells in it.

I had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel with bevacizumab, and a robotic hysterectomy with lymph nodes removed. I kept on the chemo, which gave me nerve damage, heartburn, hearing loss and a bad rash. After that there was no sign of cancer, and I stayed on bevacizumab. A few months later it came back in my liver, so I went back on carboplatin, paclitaxel and bevacizumab, which gave me scalp pain and changed my taste, and my blood pressure went up. It kept growing, in my liver and lymph nodes near it, and maybe in my pelvis, so I switched to pembrolizumab and lenvatinib. My platelets and blood counts dropped badly and I needed transfusions, and I have tendinitis in my left arm and a stiff neck. I had part of my liver removed. My treatment was stopped recently because it grew again.

I'm more tired and get winded with everyday things, but I'm on my feet all day. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I get fevers and chills at night, and last night I had a high fever. I have a dry cough that's better, some bloating, and the numbness in my feet is worse. I also have high blood pressure from the bevacizumab, a hiatal hernia, high cholesterol, an underactive thyroid, arthritis and osteoporosis.
