I'm a man with prostate cancer, found about seven years ago. I had my prostate removed. Afterwards my PSA kept rising, and a scan at Mayo Clinic that fall didn't show where the cancer was.

They planned radiation, but I started hormone treatment instead, Eligard injections, which gave me odd dreams and hot flashes. I met with the radiation doctors and decided against radiation. My PSA went down to almost nothing. My last injection was about four and a half years ago, and my PSA has been slowly rising since.

A scan two years ago showed no spread to my bones. PSMA scans at the NIH found it in lymph nodes at the back of my belly and near my diaphragm, and the later scan showed those had grown, with a new one in my pelvis. Nothing in my bones or organs.
