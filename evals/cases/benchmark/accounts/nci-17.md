I'm 40. I was diagnosed with a neuroendocrine cancer of the bladder, a large cell kind, about two years ago. I had chemo with carboplatin and etoposide plus atezolizumab. It shrank at first, then grew.

While they were checking why I was dizzy, they found a large meningioma, a tumor on the lining of my brain, and I had surgery to remove most of it. Later a mass at the top of my bladder and lymph nodes in my pelvis were growing, so I had robotic surgery to take out part of my bladder and the lymph nodes in my pelvis. A few months later it came back in my left pelvis and blocked my left kidney, so I have a tube draining that kidney through my back.
