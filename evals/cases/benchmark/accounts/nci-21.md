I'm 75. I have nerve damage in my hands and feet, and nobody knows why. I have cancer of the urethra, a mucinous adenocarcinoma. It was found after I was in the hospital because the stents in both my ureters were blocked and my kidneys were failing. After the stents were replaced, they took a sample from inside my bladder.

At first they weren't sure whether it started in my urinary tract or my bowel, but they decided it started in my urinary tract. I had a big operation to remove my bladder and other organs in my pelvis. I had one dose of FOLFOX chemo and couldn't tolerate it. After the chemo the cancer grew, in lymph nodes in the middle of my chest.
