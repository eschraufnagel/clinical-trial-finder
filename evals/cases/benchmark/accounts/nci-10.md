Account of nci-10, from its patient_summary in data/benchmark/trialbench/vignettes.json, by KNOWING.md.

I'm 48. I have stomach cancer at the junction with my esophagus. When it was found about nine months ago it had already spread to my liver.

I had chemo with fluorouracil, leucovorin and oxaliplatin, with nivolumab, for about three months, until the spots in my liver grew. Then I had ramucirumab with paclitaxel for about three months, and it was stopped because there were new spots and the old ones were bigger. Since then I've been on pembrolizumab while my oncologist looks for a clinical trial.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me.
