I'm 47. I have cervical cancer, a squamous kind. It showed up as a swollen lymph node in my neck about three years ago, and I had surgery on my neck to take out lymph nodes. By then it was in my neck and pelvis.

I had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel with pembrolizumab, then stayed on pembrolizumab. It came back after about four months, so I went back on the same chemo. When that stopped working I had tisotumab vedotin, and after that radiation to my pelvis. Then it spread to my liver and a little to my lungs, and I had carboplatin and paclitaxel with Avastin. During the last treatment I had a bad reaction to the platinum chemo: my face swelled up and went red, and my chest felt tight. It has grown again since.

My belly is swollen, I fill up quickly, and I have some discomfort under my right ribs. My weight is steady. I'm a bit tired but don't need naps, and until recently I was still playing tennis. My fingertips and toes are a little numb. I don't have chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, swelling, rashes or changes in my vision. My blood work today was fine.
