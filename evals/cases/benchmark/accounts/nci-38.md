I'm 45. I have lung cancer, an adenocarcinoma in my right lung, found about a year and a half ago, with lymph nodes in my chest. I had chemo with cisplatin and etoposide with radiation, and it shrank. Then I had durvalumab for a few months, until fluid built up around my right lung and the tumor grew a little. The fluid had cancer cells in it.

They found a spot in the right front part of my brain, and I had focused radiation to it. Then I was in a clinical trial of durvalumab and olaparib for about seven months, until it grew and the fluid around my lung increased, and I was taken off the trial.

I have pain on the right side of my chest that's getting worse. I take oxycodone through the day and at night, and I can only sleep a couple of hours. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have diabetes and high cholesterol, and I had my appendix out.
