I'm 80. I had a squamous cell skin cancer on my right forearm, removed about a year ago. A few months later I could feel lumps under my right arm, and a biopsy showed the skin cancer had spread there. The biopsy site got infected, and I was in the hospital for four days.

I had radiation under my arm. Then spots showed up in both lungs, and I had cemiplimab for a few months. The latest scan shows the lump under my arm and the spots in my lungs have grown.

The lump under my arm hurts, more when I exert myself. I look after myself but can't work, and I'm up more than half the day. I have anxiety and acid reflux, and I had prostate cancer about ten years ago, treated with radiation.
