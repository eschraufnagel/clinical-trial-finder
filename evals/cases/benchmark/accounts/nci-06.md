I'm 39. I was diagnosed with breast cancer that had already spread when they found it: to lymph nodes under my right arm, my lungs and my spine.

I had radiation to my spine. First I had a hormone pill with trastuzumab and lapatinib, then fulvestrant with trastuzumab. Then the cancer got worse in my organs and I became very sick, and those treatments were stopped. Now I've had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel.
