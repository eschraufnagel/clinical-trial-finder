I'm 60. I have head and neck cancer, a squamous kind, that started in my left tonsil. When it was found about a year ago it was in my tonsil, lymph nodes in my neck and two small spots in my lungs.

I had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel with pembrolizumab for about six months. The tonsil and lung spots shrank, but a lymph node in my neck kept growing, so I had radiation to it. Then I had pembrolizumab alone for a couple of months, but it was stopped because it inflamed my lungs. Since then I've been on cetuximab, and the lung spots have stayed the same.

I'm a retired teacher. I can do everything I did before, and I exercise.
