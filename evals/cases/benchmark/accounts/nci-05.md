I'm 75. I was diagnosed with triple-negative breast cancer about a year and a half ago. It was growing fast, so I started with pembrolizumab and chemo, carboplatin and paclitaxel and then doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide, before anything else.

About nine months ago it spread to my bones and I had a fracture. I had radiation there and injections of denosumab for my bones. After that I took Xeloda, and then sacituzumab govitecan.
