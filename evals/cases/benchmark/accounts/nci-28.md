I'm 55. I have a rare T-cell lymphoma of the intestine, called MEITL. It was found about nine months ago, after my small bowel became blocked, and it had spread to lymph nodes in my belly.

I had chemo with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and prednisone, which didn't work, and then chemo with ifosfamide, carboplatin and etoposide, and it grew. It's still in my bowel wall and the lymph nodes in my belly. It isn't in my brain or my blood.

I look after myself but can't work, and I'm up more than half the day. My eating and weight are getting better. I have type 2 diabetes and have had surgery on my belly before.
