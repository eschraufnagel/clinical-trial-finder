Account of nci-03, from its patient_summary in data/benchmark/trialbench/vignettes.json, by KNOWING.md.

I'm 70. I was diagnosed with endometrial cancer, a serous kind, about seven years ago. I had a hysterectomy with my ovaries and tubes taken out and lymph nodes removed, then radiation, then chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel.

It came back about three years ago. I had carboplatin and paclitaxel again, and it grew again. Then I had pembrolizumab with lenvatinib for about five months, and that stopped working. This summer I was in a clinical trial of a drug called DB1303 for a couple of months, and the cancer grew on that too.

My recent blood work was normal. I can do everything I did before, and I don't have any new complaints. I have a lot of fluid in my belly that's supposed to be drained, but it doesn't bother me.
