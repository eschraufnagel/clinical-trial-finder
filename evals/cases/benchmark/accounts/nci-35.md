I'm a man with prostate cancer, found about twelve years ago. I had radiation seeds then. My PSA rose over the years, and about four years ago it came back in my prostate, and I had another kind of radiation there.

Last year a scan showed it back again in my prostate, and there was no more radiation or surgery they could offer, so I started hormone treatment on and off. My urine stream got slower, and because of a narrowing in my urethra I now have a catheter through my belly into my bladder. My PSA went down to almost nothing on it. I stopped the hormone treatment this past winter, and my PSA has been rising again since.

Scans this year show it in my prostate, possibly growing toward the opening of my bladder, and some small lymph nodes in my pelvis they weren't sure about. An MRI showed a spot on my liver was a hemangioma, and my bone scan was clear. I've just joined a scan study at the NIH.
