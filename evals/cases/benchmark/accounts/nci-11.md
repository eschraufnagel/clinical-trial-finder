Account of nci-11, from its patient_summary in data/benchmark/trialbench/vignettes.json, by KNOWING.md.

I'm 59. I have non-small cell lung cancer, an adenocarcinoma, diagnosed about two years ago in my right lung and lymph nodes in my chest. I had chemo with cisplatin and radiation at the same time, then durvalumab for a year.

About a year ago a small new spot showed up in my left lung, and a biopsy showed the cancer had come back. I took alectinib for about six months, then switched to lorlatinib, which I'm still on. Of the spots in my left lung, one is staying the same and one is growing very slowly. My oncologist is keeping me on it while we look into clinical trials.

I work part-time as an office manager, and I walked a 5K last month. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me.
