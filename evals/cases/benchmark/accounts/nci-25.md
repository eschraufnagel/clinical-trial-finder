I'm 63. I have a T-cell lymphoma, a peripheral T-cell lymphoma, diagnosed about two years ago. When it was found it was in my lymph nodes and my bone marrow.

I had chemo with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and prednisone, and it shrank, but it grew back about six months later. Then I had chemo with ifosfamide, carboplatin and etoposide, which didn't work. Then I had mogamulizumab, which helped my skin and lymph nodes for a short while, but it grew again within three months. Now it's growing in lymph nodes at the back of my belly and in my chest. It isn't in my brain or my blood.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have high blood pressure and mild kidney disease. My blood work is okay.
