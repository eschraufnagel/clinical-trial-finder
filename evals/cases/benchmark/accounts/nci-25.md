I'm 63. I have a T-cell lymphoma, a peripheral T-cell lymphoma, diagnosed about two years ago. When it was found it was in my lymph nodes and my bone marrow.

I had CHOP chemo, and it shrank, but it grew back about six months later. Then I had ICE chemo, which didn't work. Then I had mogamulizumab, which helped my skin and lymph nodes for a short while, but it grew again within three months. Now it's growing in lymph nodes at the back of my belly and in my chest.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have high blood pressure and mild kidney disease. My blood work is okay.
