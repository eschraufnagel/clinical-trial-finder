Account of nci-41, from its patient_summary in data/benchmark/trialbench/vignettes.json, by KNOWING.md.

I'm 80. I had melanoma on my scalp, a bleeding spot, found about three years ago. I had surgery to remove it and the nearest lymph node, then pembrolizumab.

Within a few months it came back near there, and I had surgery on my salivary gland and lymph nodes in my neck, and finished pembrolizumab about a year later. Last summer a scan showed it back again, and I had more lymph nodes removed from my neck. Then I had nivolumab with ipilimumab, and afterwards there was a new spot in my liver and a new one on the skin of my right neck.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have high blood pressure and high cholesterol, and I had prostate cancer and my prostate removed. I've also had a hernia repaired and my tonsils out. I have no allergies.
