I'm 61. I have celiac disease and iron-deficiency anemia. I have a T-cell lymphoma of the intestine, called enteropathy-associated T-cell lymphoma. It was found about ten months ago, after a hole in my small bowel, and it had spread to lymph nodes in my belly.

I had chemo with cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and prednisone, which didn't work, and then brentuximab vedotin, and it grew. Some of it is still in my bowel wall and the lymph nodes in my belly. It isn't in my brain or my blood.

I look after myself but can't work, and I'm up more than half the day. I'm getting better since my surgery.
