I'm 75. I had penile cancer, a squamous kind, about two years ago, and had part of my penis removed. A couple of months later a lymph node in my right groin got bigger, and a biopsy showed the cancer there.

I had chemo with paclitaxel, ifosfamide and cisplatin, and a scan afterwards showed it had grown in the lymph nodes in both groins and my pelvis. Then I had chemo with 5-fluorouracil and mitomycin together with radiation. After that the lymph nodes grew again and it spread to my chest.
