I'm 38. I was diagnosed with cervical cancer, an adenocarcinoma, about three and a half years ago. I had a hysterectomy, then chemo and radiation afterwards because it had grown into the tissue beside my uterus.

About two and a half years ago it came back in my lungs. I had chemo with cisplatin and paclitaxel, plus pembrolizumab and bevacizumab, then stayed on pembrolizumab for about a year. It grew in my lung, so I had a break from treatment for a couple of months. Then I had tisotumab vedotin for about nine months, until a scan this summer showed new spots in my liver.

My oncologist is starting topotecan while I look for a clinical trial. I work full-time. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me.
