I'm 58. I have a T-cell lymphoma, an angioimmunoblastic T-cell lymphoma, diagnosed about a year and a half ago. My lymph nodes all over were swollen and my spleen was enlarged.

I had brentuximab vedotin with chemo, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin and prednisone, and it went away. It came back nine months later. Then I had romidepsin, which held it for a while, and then it grew. Now my lymph nodes are swollen again, and I have what they call B symptoms: fevers, night sweats or weight loss. It isn't in my blood.

I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. I have an underactive thyroid, and I once had a kind of anemia where my immune system attacked my red blood cells, but that's not active now. I don't have an infection.
