I'm 57. I have anal cancer, a squamous kind, diagnosed about a year and a half ago. I first had chemo with capecitabine and mitomycin together with radiation.

About a year ago it spread to my liver. I had chemo with carboplatin and paclitaxel for a few months, but it was stopped because I had a reaction to the infusion. Then I had nivolumab for about six months until it grew in my liver. After that I was in a clinical trial of a new immunotherapy called GIGA-564 for a few months, and the cancer in my liver grew on that too.

I'm self-employed and work about 20 hours a week, and I keep house. I can do light work, but heavy things tire me. My oncologist may start me on chemo with fluorouracil, leucovorin and oxaliplatin if I don't find a trial soon.
