I'm 58. I have a cancer called urachal adenocarcinoma, which grows from a leftover structure at the top of the bladder. It was found almost four years ago, and a colonoscopy then was clear. I had robotic surgery to take out part of my bladder.

About a year ago it came back at the top of my bladder and in the lining of my belly, and it had spread to a lymph node in my left pelvis and my belly wall. I had FOLFOX chemo, then surgery to remove as much as they could, including more of my bladder, with heated chemo washed through my belly. Recently a biopsy showed it has come back in the lining of my belly.
