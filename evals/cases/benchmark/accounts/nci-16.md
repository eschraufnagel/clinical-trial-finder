Account of nci-16, from its patient_summary in data/benchmark/trialbench/vignettes.json, by KNOWING.md.

I'm 73. I have high cholesterol, and a kidney condition called medullary sponge kidney that gives me kidney stones; I had surgery for a large stone about eight years ago. I was diagnosed with small cell cancer of the bladder about three years ago, mixed with another kind of bladder cancer.

I had chemo with cisplatin and etoposide before any surgery, and it went away. It came back in my bladder about a year ago and had spread to my liver, and maybe to a bone in my lower back. I had two more rounds of cisplatin and etoposide, and it grew. Then I had pembrolizumab for about eight months, with breaks because it inflamed my lungs. A recent scan showed the spots in my liver have clearly grown.
