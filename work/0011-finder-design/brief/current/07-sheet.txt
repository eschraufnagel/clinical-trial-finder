For your oncologist

A list of trials to ask about, from a search of ClinicalTrials.gov as it stood in 2026-10 and a short set of questions. It doesn't say whether any trial suits this person. Their oncologist and each trial's team decide that.

Questions that would settle the most of these trials
Has the cancer spread to the lining of the brain or spinal cord? 7 of these 10 trials ask about this.
In the last 2 years, have you needed medicine that calms the immune system, such as steroids or immunosuppressants, for an autoimmune disease like lupus or Crohn's? Thyroid hormone, insulin and skin creams don't count. 2 of these 10 trials ask about this.
Do you have active hepatitis B or C? 2 of these 10 trials ask about this.
Did testing find an EGFR mutation? 1 of these 10 trials asks about this.
Did testing find a KRAS mutation? 1 of these 10 trials asks about this.
What was the PD-L1 test result? 1 of these 10 trials asks about this.
